How to Make a Monoprint

1
Get Your Materials
  • Black oil pastel
  • Tracing paper
  • White paper
  • Pencil
  • Textured objects
2
Color the Tracing Paper
  • Take tracing paper
  • Draw on it with black oil pastel
  • Cover one side completely
3
Add Textured Objects
  • Place textured objects on white paper
  • Arrange them in a pattern
  • Try leaves, string, or netting
4
Put It All Together
  • Place black pastel tracing paper on top
  • Black side DOWN
  • Press with pencil or spoon
  • Rub all over the paper
5
Reveal Your Print
  • Lift tracing paper
  • Remove objects
  • See your monoprint!

Helpful Tips

Use Lots of Black Pastel

Apply black oil pastel thickly for better prints

Press Hard

Use firm pressure when rubbing to transfer black color
